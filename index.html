Questions vets ask

Where are my notes stored?

Saved only in your browser. Text sent for AI drafting is not stored by PawScribe.

Do I need an account to use the free templates?

No. The templates and the note builder work with no account and no install.

Can I edit the draft before saving?

Yes. Every draft is editable, and every AI draft is marked DRAFT until you review it.

What does the AI draft do with my notes?

AI drafting connects soon — the free templates work now. When it connects, text sent for AI drafting is not stored by PawScribe.

Does PawScribe diagnose or prescribe?

No. PawScribe drafts notes; it does not diagnose or treat. Always review before saving.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Pro and Clinic are monthly PayPal subscriptions with no contract. Cancel anytime; the workspace unlock is stored locally in your browser.
