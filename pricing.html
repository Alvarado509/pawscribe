Pricing questions

Where are my notes stored?

Saved only in your browser. Text sent for AI drafting is not stored by PawScribe.

What does the AI draft do with my notes?

AI drafting connects soon — the free templates work now. When it connects, your rough notes are sent to the drafting service to build an editable SOAP draft, and the text is not stored by PawScribe.

What happens after I subscribe?

PayPal confirms your subscription and your plan is saved in this browser, so the workspace unlocks your Pro or Clinic note limit right away. Your subscription itself lives in your PayPal account — PawScribe never sees your card details.

How do I cancel?

Cancel anytime from your PayPal account, under your subscriptions. There is no contract and no cancellation fee. Your plan stays active until the end of the billing period you already paid for.

What are the 5 free AI notes?

On the Free plan you can turn rough notes, dictation, or a recorded consultation into 5 editable SOAP drafts per month, at no cost and with no card. Templates and the note builder are free without any limit.

What about refunds?

There is no contract — you can cancel anytime. Charges are handled by PayPal, so any refund request goes through PayPal under their terms.
