About PawScribe

An AI scribe for veterinarians. Less time typing notes, more time with patients.

What PawScribe is

PawScribe turns messy exam notes into clean, structured SOAP notes. The vet pastes rough notes, dictates, or records a consultation, and gets an editable SOAP draft — Subjective, Objective, Assessment, Plan — in seconds. The vet always reviews and edits the draft before it goes into the record.

It works entirely in the browser. There is nothing to install, no account to create, and the free templates and note builder are free to use as they are.

Who it is for

PawScribe is built for independent veterinary clinics in the United States. There are 130,415 veterinarians in the US, and 93.9% of vet practices are independently owned. Most of them are small teams without an IT department — so the tool has to work the moment you open it, with no setup and no training.

The problem

Documentation eats the veterinary day. Vets spend 2–3 hours per day on documentation, and one SOAP note takes 10–15 minutes by hand. That is time taken from patients, from the team, and from going home on time. PawScribe exists to shrink that part of the day: the draft is ready in seconds, and the vet spends their time checking it, not typing it from scratch.

How it works

1

Paste what you jotted down, dictate it, or record the consultation.

2

PawScribe organises your input into Subjective, Objective, Assessment, and Plan in seconds.

3

Review, edit, and use it

You check every line, fix what is wrong, and copy the finished note into your record. The vet stays in charge of the record.

How PawScribe compares

AI scribes for vets are a new category, and the standard price is around $99 per vet per month. PawScribe Pro is $49 per vet per month — half the category standard — with a free plan that includes templates, the note builder, and 5 AI notes a month. There is no install and no account: the free tools work the moment you open the page.

The category itself is real and growing: Scribenote raised an $8.2M seed round led by Andreessen Horowitz (a16z), VetRec went through Y Combinator (S23), and Abridge — the same idea for human doctors — is valued at $5.3B. PawScribe is the independent, browser-first option in that category.

What PawScribe does not do

  • No diagnosis — PawScribe drafts notes, it does not tell you what is wrong with a patient.
  • No treatment advice — it does not recommend treatments or medications.
  • No drug-dosage calculator — all dosages are the vet's responsibility.
  • No PIMS integration in v1 — notes are copied from PawScribe into your practice system by hand.

AI drafts always need review. PawScribe is a drafting tool, not a clinician.
