About

Notes, finished in the exam room.

PawScribe is a scribe for veterinarians — not a practice system, not a diagnostic tool.

What PawScribe is

PawScribe turns rough notes, dictation, or a recorded consultation into an editable SOAP draft. The vet reviews the draft, edits it, and decides what goes into the medical record. It works in a browser on any device — phone, tablet, or exam-room computer — with nothing to install.

The free tier is the whole point of the product: SOAP templates and a note builder that work with no account. The paid plans add AI drafting, custom templates, full history, and discharge instructions.

Who it is for

Independent veterinarians in the US. There are 130,415 veterinarians in the US, and 93.9% of vet practices are independently owned — mostly small teams where the vet does the notes alone, after hours. PawScribe is built for that vet: one person, one browser tab, one finished note per patient.

Why the problem matters

Vets spend 2–3 hours per day on documentation, and one SOAP note takes 10–15 minutes to write by hand. Average vet income is $163,014 a year, and one vet-hour earns about $288 in revenue. The typing is the bottleneck — not the thinking.

How it works, honestly

PawScribe is a static website. Your notes and templates are saved only in your browser (localStorage). There is no PawScribe server and no account database.

AI drafting connects through a small proxy (a Cloudflare Worker) when it is switched on. Until then, the free templates and the note builder work now, and AI drafting is labelled as coming soon. When AI drafting connects, text sent for drafting is not stored by PawScribe.

PawScribe drafts notes; it does not diagnose or treat. Always review before saving.

The category is proven

The AI scribe idea is proven in veterinary medicine and beyond. Scribenote, a veterinary scribe, raised $8.2M led by Andreessen Horowitz (a16z). Abridge — the same idea for human doctors — is valued at $5.3B. PawScribe brings that idea to the independent vet at $49 a month, with no install and no setup.
