Terms of Service

Short, plain terms. Using PawScribe means you agree to them.

The service

PawScribe is a browser-based tool that helps veterinarians write SOAP notes. It provides free templates and a note builder, and — on paid plans — AI drafting, custom templates, full note history, and discharge instructions.

PawScribe drafts notes; it does not diagnose or treat. Always review before saving. You are responsible for the accuracy of every note you file.

Plans

  • Free — $0. SOAP templates, note builder, 5 AI notes per month.
  • Pro — $49 per vet, per month. 400 AI notes per month, custom templates, full note history, discharge instructions.
  • Clinic — $119 per month. Up to 5 vets, 2,000 AI notes per month, shared templates.

All pricing in USD. Features may change; the plan you pay for is the plan described on the pricing page at the time of purchase.

Billing

Billing is via PayPal subscriptions. Your card details are handled by PayPal; PawScribe never sees or stores them. Your subscriptions are verified at checkout; the workspace unlock is stored locally in your browser.

Plans renew monthly until cancelled. If a payment fails, the workspace returns to the Free plan.

Cancellation

Cancel anytime from PayPal, with no fee and no notice period. Cancellation stops the next renewal; the current month is not refunded. After cancellation, your notes stay in your browser until you delete them.

Clinical disclaimer

PawScribe drafts notes; it does not diagnose or treat. Always review before saving. AI drafts can contain errors, omit findings, or misread your notes. Never file a draft you have not reviewed. PawScribe does not calculate drug dosages and does not give treatment advice. Clinical decisions are yours alone.

No warranty on AI drafts

AI drafts are provided as-is, with no warranty of accuracy, completeness, or fitness for any purpose. PawScribe is not liable for errors in a draft, for notes filed from a draft, or for any clinical outcome. The templates and the note builder are tools; the medical record is your professional responsibility.

Your data

Notes are saved only in your browser. Text sent for AI drafting is not stored by PawScribe. See the Privacy Policy for the full picture.

Fair use

Do not abuse the service: no automated bulk use, no reselling access, no attempt to extract another user’s notes or keys. Abuse may end access without refund.
