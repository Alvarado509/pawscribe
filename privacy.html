Privacy

Your notes stay yours

Saved only in your browser. Text sent for AI drafting is not stored by PawScribe.

The short version

Saved only in your browser. Text sent for AI drafting is not stored by PawScribe.

PawScribe is a static website. There is no PawScribe server, no account database, and no analytics that follows you. We cannot see your notes, because they never reach us.

What is stored in your browser

Everything PawScribe saves lives in your browser’s localStorage, under keys starting with pawscribe_:

  • Your notes — note history, capped at the last 100 notes.
  • Your templates — custom templates you create.
  • Your plan — whether your browser is unlocked for Free, Pro, or Clinic.
  • Your AI quota — how many AI notes you have used this month.
  • Your API key, only if you add one — in the bring-your-own-key option, your key is stored in your browser only.
  • Your theme choice — light or dark mode.

Clearing your browser data deletes all of it. There is no copy anywhere else.

AI drafting

AI drafting connects soon — the free templates work now. When it connects, the text you send for drafting goes to the AI provider through a small proxy, and the draft comes back to your browser. Text sent for AI drafting is not stored by PawScribe. The AI provider processes the text to produce the draft; PawScribe keeps no copy of it.

Every AI draft is marked DRAFT until you review it. PawScribe never files a note automatically.

Dictation and recording

Dictation uses your browser’s built-in Web Speech API. In Chrome, that API sends audio to Google’s servers — that is the browser’s behaviour, not ours, and it happens only when you press the microphone button. Recorded audio is captured in your browser and sent to the AI provider only when you ask for a draft.

Payments

Payments are handled by PayPal. PawScribe never sees or stores your card details. PayPal tells the site that a subscription is active; the unlock is stored locally in your browser.
