Privacy

Plain language, no fine print. Here is what happens to your data when you use PawScribe.

Your notes stay in your browser

Saved only in your browser. Text sent for AI drafting is not stored by PawScribe.

Your notes, templates, and drafts are saved in your browser's local storage, on your own device. They are not uploaded to a PawScribe server, because there is no PawScribe server holding your notes. If you clear your browser data, your saved notes are gone — keep a copy in your practice system.

What happens when you press Generate

Nothing leaves your browser until you press Generate. Only then is the text you entered sent to the AI provider so it can draft your SOAP note. The draft comes back to your browser. PawScribe does not store the text you sent.

If you dictate with your browser's built-in speech recognition, that is Chrome's Web Speech API: your audio is sent to Google's servers to be transcribed. That is how the browser feature works — it is browser behaviour, not something PawScribe does or controls. If you would rather not send audio to Google, type or paste your notes instead.

No account, no tracking, no selling data

  • No account — you never create a login or give us your name or email to use PawScribe.
  • No tracking — we do not follow you across the web and we do not run analytics that identify you.
  • No selling data — we do not sell your notes or any information about you to anyone.
  • No ads in the workspace.

Payments

Payments are handled by PayPal. When you subscribe, you pay on PayPal's site, and PayPal stores your payment details. We never see your card details. Your plan is saved in your browser only, so the workspace on this device knows you are on Pro or Clinic.
